# Connector

Your future closest friends are just outside your circle. Don't let them stay strangers.

## Problem statement

**Who:** Wharton MBA students whose social life has settled into a few familiar circles: their cluster, the two or three clubs they're active in, and the people in their classes. They want to meet people they'd genuinely click with, often people quite different from them, whom they just haven't crossed paths with.

**When:** On a free lunch hour, weekday evening or weekend while everyone's on campus, especially in Q2 and the spring semester, once routines have set in.

**What they do today:** Mostly nothing. Some try talking to new people at parties or club events, but approaching a stranger feels awkward, so people stay comfortable and default to their own group. The want is there; the nudge isn't.
